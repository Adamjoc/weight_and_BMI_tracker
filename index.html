Reading this honestly

The two lines move in lockstep — BMI is just your weight divided by 2.958, so it can't tell you anything the weight doesn't. It's here because clinicians use it, not because it adds information.

BMI also can't distinguish muscle from fat, and takes no account of where fat sits. Waist measurement is the better single number for the visceral fat you're targeting — worth taking once a week at the navel, same time of day.

Day-to-day movement of half a kilo either way is water, salt and gut contents, not fat. The line that matters is the one drawn over a fortnight.
