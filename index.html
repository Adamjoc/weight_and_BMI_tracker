    Reading this honestly

    Day-to-day movement of half a kilo either way is water, salt and gut contents, not fat. The line that matters is the one drawn over a fortnight.

    Weigh the same way every time — morning, after the loo, before food, minimal clothing. Readings ticked as not fasted show as hollow dots and sit outside the trend, because clothes and a meal are worth well over a kilo and would otherwise look like real change.

    BMI is just your weight divided by 2.958, so it can't tell you anything the weight doesn't. Waist is the better number. It tracks visceral fat, which is what you're actually trying to shift, and it moves more slowly but more honestly than the scale.

    Roughly one centimetre of waist goes with each kilogram lost in this range. Eight kilograms takes you from 102 to about 94 — the working target. 86 needs closer to sixteen, so treat it as next year's line, not this one's.
